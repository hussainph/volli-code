A **Browser Tab** shows a web page inside Home or a Ticket view. You can open
one yourself, or show a page a Session opened while it worked.

## Open a Browser Tab

1. In Home or a Ticket view, select the **Open a tab** (`+`) control.
2. Choose **Browser**.
3. In the **Address** field, enter a URL and press Return.

A bare hostname uses HTTPS. Local addresses such as `localhost:3000` use
HTTP. Local file URLs are not supported. Use **Back**, **Forward**, and
**Reload** to navigate, or **Toggle DevTools** to inspect the page.

A project can have up to 32 person-created tabs. Each Session can own up to
six hidden agent tabs; tabs you show no longer count against that hidden-tab
limit. Close or reuse a tab when you reach the limit.

Browser Tabs run sandboxed from the rest of the app. Treat page content as
untrusted, even inside Volli. Pages send requests to their websites, and what
you click or type can be sent there too. See
[Local data and network access](/reference/data-and-privacy/).

## Show a page a Session opened

A page a Session opens starts hidden. Its card appears in the chat, and its
page is listed in the Activity Island above the composer.

- Choose **Open beside chat** to show the page to the right of its owning chat
  in a [tab group](/guides/split-view/). If the chat already belongs to a group,
  Volli splits that chat's pane to make room. If the page is already in the
  same group, Volli brings it forward.
- In the Activity Island, **Show tab** brings an already-visible page forward
  where it is.
- In the tab's menu, choose **Hide to Island** to remove a Session's page from
  the strip without closing it. Open it again from the Activity Island.
- Choose **Close** on the transcript card or **Close tab** in the Activity
  Island to close the page.

Showing or hiding a page leaves its owner and current hold unchanged. The
Session can keep using it. A shown tab stays available after its Session ends,
until you close it or quit Volli. When a delegated subagent finishes, Volli
closes its unheld hidden tabs; tabs held by you or another Session stay open.

## Take or return control

A colored dot beside the address identifies the Session holding the tab.
Hover over the dot to see its name. A matching Session cursor shows where it
acts on the page.

- Choose **Take over** to take control now. Volli tells the Session that it no
  longer holds the tab.
- Choose **Ask to leave** to ask the Session to release the tab when it is
  safe. It keeps control until it releases the tab.
- When you hold the tab, choose **Hand back** to make it available again. A
  Session can acquire it on a later action.

You can click or type into the page while a Session holds it. Page input,
address edits, and navigation controls do not automatically take control;
use **Take over** when you want the Session to stop driving.

<Aside type="note">
  A Session can see tabs it opened and tabs you opened, but not unrelated
  Sessions' tabs. A tab's owner is separate from whoever currently holds it.
  Taking control does not change ownership.
</Aside>

## Replay browser activity

On a browser card in the transcript, choose **Replay** to open **Browser
replay**. The replay shows recorded steps across the Session's own tabs,
including outcomes, page addresses, and retained pictures. It can remain
available after the live tab closes or Volli relaunches.

- Use **Previous step**, **Next step**, or the arrow keys to move one step.
- Drag the timeline to scrub. `Page Up` and `Page Down` move ten steps;
  `Home` and `End` jump to the first or latest step.
- Stay on the latest step to follow new activity. Scrub back to keep reading
  an earlier step while the Session works.

A step without a new picture can show an earlier frame labeled **As of
step**. **Picture unavailable** means that frame is no longer retained.
Recordings have retention limits, so older steps or whole recordings can
expire. A replay is step-by-step evidence, not a continuous video or a way to
repeat actions on the live page.
