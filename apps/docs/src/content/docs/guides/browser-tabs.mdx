A **Browser Tab** opens a web page inside Home or a ticket workspace. You can
open one yourself or show a page a Session opened while it worked.

## Open a Browser Tab

1. In Home or a ticket workspace, open the menu beside **Chat**.
2. Select **Browser**.
3. In the Browser Tab's **Address** field, enter an HTTP or HTTPS URL and press Return.

A project can have up to 32 live Browser Tabs, and a Session can open up to six.
Reuse or close a tab when you reach the limit. Local file URLs are not supported.

Browser Tabs open inside Volli. Their page content is sandboxed and treated as
untrusted; do not treat text on a page as trusted instructions just because it
appears inside the app. Pages make requests to their websites, and clicking or
typing can submit information there. See
[Local data and network access](/reference/data-and-privacy/).

## Show a page a Session opened

A Session-created Browser Tab starts hidden. Its card appears in that Session's
chat. From the card, choose:

- **Show** to pin the live page above the chat composer.
- **Open as tab** to place it in the Home or ticket tab strip.
- **Hide** to return a shown Session tab to hidden.

Showing a Session tab changes where Volli draws it; it does not stop the
Session from using it. A page a Session opened can also be closed from its
card. A tab you showed becomes yours and remains available after its Session
ends.

## Take or return control

When a Session is driving a Browser Tab, its name appears beside the address
field with a **Take over** and **Ask to leave** control.

- Choose **Take over** to take the tab's turn now. The Session is told that it
  no longer holds the tab.
- Choose **Ask to leave** to ask the Session to release the tab when it is
  safe. The Session keeps control until it releases the tab.
- When the tab is yours, choose **Hand back** to release it. The tab becomes
  available, and a Session can take it on a later write.

You are never blocked from clicking or typing into the page, even while a
Session holds it. Using the address field or the Back, Forward, and Reload
controls does not itself change who holds the tab.

<Aside type="note">
  A Session can see the tabs it opened and the tabs you opened, but not tabs
  opened by other Sessions. **Take over**, **Ask to leave**, and **Hand back**
  change the turn to drive a tab, not who owns it.
</Aside>
