Use **Split view** to show several tabs at once by grouping them. A **tab
group** is two or more panes, side by side or stacked, and each pane holds its
own stack of tabs. Home and each Ticket view keep their own groups, and a tab
cannot move from Home to a Ticket view, or between Ticket views.

The top tab strip shows a group as one chip. Each segment of the chip is the
front tab of one pane. Select a segment to show the group with that pane
focused. Select any other tab to show it on its own; select the chip again to
return to the group exactly as you left it.

## Make a group

- Press `⌘\` to open an empty pane to the right of the tab in front, or `⇧⌘\`
  to open one below it. In a group, this splits the focused pane.
- Drag a tab onto the left or right edge of another tab in the top strip.
- Drag a tab, Session, or file onto an edge of the plane.

An empty pane offers **New chat**, **New terminal**, **New browser**, **Open
file…**, and **Close pane**.

## Open tabs into a group

While a group is shown, anything new you open joins the focused pane, in
front: a new chat or terminal, a new Browser Tab, or a file opened with `⌘P`
or from a file list. Each pane has its own tab strip, so you can switch,
close, rename, and reorder its tabs there.

## Move tabs between panes

Drag a tab from a pane's strip:

- Onto another pane's center or strip to move it into that pane.
- Onto an edge of a pane to split there and place it in a new pane.
- Onto the top strip to take it out of the group.

The highlight previews where the item will land. A pane emptied by a move
closes, and a group left with one pane goes back to ordinary tabs.

## Focus, resize, and ungroup

Select a pane or one of its tabs to focus it. The focused pane decides which
tab is active and what the rail describes. Drag a divider to resize panes, and
use `⌃⌘` plus an arrow key to move focus to an adjacent pane.

Right-click a group's chip to see every tab in it, pane by pane, with each
pane's front tab checked. Choose a tab to bring it forward, or choose
**Ungroup** to split the group back into ordinary tabs. Closing a pane moves
its tabs into the pane beside it. Ungrouping and closing panes never close a
tab.
