Open a ticket to update its scope and properties, discuss the work, or review
what changed. The **Ticket Body** holds the ticket's Markdown scope. The
**Now** rail shows its **Properties** and Session history.

## Update ticket details

Select the ticket title to edit it. Edit the **Ticket Body** in its tab; changes
autosave. In **Now → Properties**, change the ticket's **Status**, **Priority**,
or **Labels**. A status change also moves the ticket on the board.

Labels belong to the project. Use **Add label** to choose an existing label,
or type a new name and choose the create option to add it to the project's
label list.

## Comments

The **Activity** feed appears below the Ticket Body. Add a comment in its
composer and select **Comment** or press `⌘Enter`. Comments render as Markdown.

You can edit or delete your own comments from their **Edit comment** and
**Delete comment** controls. An edited comment shows an “edited” mark beside
its time. Comments written by a Session have no edit or delete controls for you.

## History

The **Activity** feed combines comments with ticket events, such as status,
priority, label, and Ticket Body changes. When several events are grouped into
one row, expand it to inspect each event.

## Archiving

To take a ticket off the board without deleting it, open its board-card context
menu and select **Archive**. The ticket, its history, branch, and worktree stay
available. Use the archive control in the board header to open the project's
archived tickets.

Select **Restore** to return a ticket to the board. **Delete permanently**
removes an archived ticket and its full history, and cannot be undone. Archiving
a ticket does not remove its worktree. See [Clean up worktrees](/guides/clean-up-worktrees/)
for removal and retention behavior.
