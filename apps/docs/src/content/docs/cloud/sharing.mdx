Every Member of a Workspace sees its whole board: every project, ticket,
comment and label. Sessions follow two rules:

- **Ticket work is shared.** A Session started on a ticket, including an
  Automation's Run, is visible to every Member from the start. So is ticket
  history you [moved from this Mac](/cloud/move-projects/).
- **Chats without a ticket are private.** A project chat or Board Session is
  visible only to you until you share it.

A subagent has its parent's visibility. A private Session's existence is
hidden too: teammates do not see it in lists, and a link to it reads as not
found.

## Share a private chat

Sharing is one-way: a shared Session cannot be made private again.

1. Right-click the Session in the sidebar, on Home, or on its tab, or open
   the **⋯** menu at the top left of the chat.
2. Choose **Share with workspace…**.
3. Review **Share with workspace?** and select **Share**.

Everyone in the Workspace can then read the chat and its subagents. The
lock beside the Session changes to the team mark.

Only the Session's owner sees **Share with workspace…**, and only while the
Session is private. A subagent is shared with its parent and has no Share
action of its own.

## What teammates see

On Session rows, tabs and the chat header:

- the owner's initials, on someone else's Session;
- a lock for a private Session, or a team mark for one the Workspace can
  read.

A teammate who opens a shared Session reads its history and follows its
reply as it streams. They cannot send to it, stop it or answer its
questions: in place of the composer they see **Only** the owner's name
**can send to this Session.** To work on the same ticket, a teammate starts
their own Session there, on their own runner.

If the owner leaves the Workspace while a turn is running, that Session
reads **Ended ·** and the owner's name, for example **Ended · Sam left**.

Some things stay with one person even inside a shared Workspace:

- **Runners.** Your runners run only your Sessions. Teammates see a runner's
  name only through a Session they can already read, such as **Running on**
  that runner.
- **Credentials.** Each person's model sign-ins and secrets stay on their
  own machines.

## Make everything shared

Owners and admins can turn on **Settings → Workspace → Sharing → Make
everything shared**. While it is on, every Member can read every Session in
the Workspace, including private chats; the team mark's label reads
**Visible to the workspace (admin setting)**.

The setting changes no Session. Turning it off makes private chats private
again; ticket Sessions and chats someone shared stay shared. Owners and
admins have no other way to read a Member's private chat.
