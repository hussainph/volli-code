A **Workspace** is a group of people, the **Members**, who share projects,
the board and ticket work. Each Member has a role. Your first sign-in with
preview access creates a Workspace with you as its only owner, named after
you, or **My workspace** if your account has no name.

Workspace controls exist only in a Volli Cloud launch. See [Volli Cloud
preview](/cloud/).

## Create and switch Workspaces

The Workspace tile at the top of the rail shows the current Workspace's
initial. Select it to open the Workspace menu.

- **Switch:** choose a Workspace from the list. The rail, board and member
  list reload for that Workspace.
- **Create:** choose **New workspace…**, enter a name of up to 64
  characters, and select **Create**. You become its owner and it opens.
- **Join with a code:** choose **Join a workspace…**. See [Accept an
  invite](#accept-an-invite).
- **Settings:** choose **Workspace settings…** to open **Settings →
  Workspace**.

Volli reopens the Workspace you used last. If you are removed from the
Workspace you are viewing, Volli shows **You're no longer a member of** that
Workspace and opens another one.

While Volli Cloud is unreachable, the menu says **Volli Cloud is offline.**
and Workspace changes are refused with **Can’t reach Volli Cloud ·
Read-only**.

## Workspace settings

Open **Settings → Workspace**.

- **Workspace** shows the **Name**, **Your role** and **Membership**. Owners
  and admins can rename the Workspace here.
- **Members** lists each person's name, email and role; **You** marks your
  own row.
- **Invites** lists pending invites.
- **Sharing** holds **Make everything shared** and **Who can invite**. See
  [Share Sessions](/cloud/sharing/).
- **Invitations to you** lists email invites waiting for you.

Workspaces cannot be deleted in the preview.

## Roles

| Action | Member | Admin | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See every project, ticket and ticket Session | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See the member list, invites and sharing settings | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Invite | As Member, when **Who can invite** is **Members** | As Member or Admin | As any role |
| Revoke an invite | Their own | Any | Any |
| Rename the Workspace, change **Sharing** settings | No | Yes | Yes |
| Change roles, remove Members | No | Yes, except owners | Yes |

**Who can invite** is **Members** by default. An admin cannot change an
owner's role, remove an owner, or make anyone an owner.

A Workspace always keeps at least one owner. To step down as the only
owner, make someone else an owner first.

## Invite people

1. In **Settings → Workspace → Members**, select **Invite**.
2. Choose **Email** or **Code**. For **Email**, enter the address.
3. Choose a **Role**. The list offers only roles you may grant.
4. Select **Create invite**.
5. Select **Copy invite** and send the copied message yourself. Volli does
   not send email.

The copied message tells the invitee how to join. Every invite expires
after 7 days.

- **A code** is single-use: the first person to use it joins.
- **An email invite** can be accepted only by someone signed in with that
  verified email address.

Pending invites appear under **Invites** with the role and whether the
invitee can join the preview. **New code** replaces the invite with a fresh
one and a new 7-day expiry; the old code stops working. **Revoke** cancels
it.

An invite depends on the person who sent it. If they leave, are removed, or
lose the right to invite at that role, the invite stops working.

## Accept an invite

- **New to the preview, with a code:** paste the code into **Invite code
  (optional)** when you sign in. See [Join the preview](/cloud/join/).
- **Already signed in, with a code:** in the Workspace menu, choose **Join a
  workspace…**, enter the code, and select **Preview**. Check the Workspace,
  role and sender it shows, then select **Join**.
- **An email invite:** sign in with the invited address. A notice names the
  person and the Workspace with a **Join** action, and the invite waits under
  **Settings → Workspace → Invitations to you** with its own **Join**.

The Workspace opens when you join.

## Preview access and the sponsor allowance

An invite can also bring the invitee into the preview. Each person can
sponsor up to **5** people this way, across all their Workspaces.

The status on an invite tells you which applies:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Can join** | The invitee already has preview access, or this invite gives it to them |
| **Needs preview access** | You have no sponsorships left, so the invitee needs access another way |

- A sponsorship is held while its invite is pending. Revoking the invite, or
  letting it expire, gives it back.
- Once someone joins with it, it stays theirs and keeps counting.
- Inviting someone who already has preview access uses none.
- Removing someone from your Workspace later does not take away their
  preview access.

A person without access who signs in with a **Needs preview access** code is
refused with **This invite doesn't include Volli Cloud preview access. Ask the
person who invited you.**, followed by the waitlist message.

## Change roles and remove Members

In **Settings → Workspace → Members**, open a row's **More** menu.

- Choose a role to change it. The menu lists only the changes you may make.
- Choose **Remove…**, then **Remove** to confirm.

A removed Member's shared work stays in the Workspace; their private chats
are hidden. Their armed Automations pause until someone
re-arms them; see [Automations in Volli Cloud](/cloud/automations/). You can
invite them back later.

## Leave a Workspace

In **Settings → Workspace**, select **Leave…** in **Membership**, then
**Leave**. Your shared work stays; your private chats are hidden, and your
armed Automations pause.

The only owner cannot leave. Their **Leave…** button is disabled and
marked **Sole owner**, even when they are the only Member.
