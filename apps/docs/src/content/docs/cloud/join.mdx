The Volli Cloud preview is invite-only. You get in one of these ways:

- **An invite code** from a teammate or from Volli, pasted when you sign in.
- **An email invite**: someone invited your address to their Workspace, and
  you sign in with that address.
- **Preview access granted to your email** by Volli, with no code needed.

Signing in without any of these puts you on the waitlist. Your local
projects are unchanged either way.

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. If you have a code, paste it into **Invite code (optional)**. Codes are
   at least 16 characters.
3. Check **I am at least 18 and agree to the Preview Agreement and
   acknowledge the Privacy notice**. The sign-in buttons stay disabled until
   you do; the links open both documents.
4. Select **Continue with GitHub** or **Continue with Google**, and finish in
   your browser. The button reads **Waiting for the browser** until you do;
   select **Cancel** to stop waiting.

Use the email address an email invite was sent to. Only a verified email at
your GitHub or Google account matches an invite.

If the code cannot be used, the whole sign-in is refused and nothing is
granted. Fix the code, or remove it, and sign in again.

## Restart into Volli Cloud

A running window never switches boards. After a sign-in with preview access,
Volli offers **Restart to open Volli Cloud** as a notice with **Restart now**
and **Later**, and as a **Board** row in **Settings → Account** until you
restart.

Restarting runs the usual quit checks. If agent turns are still running,
choose **Switch When Finished** to wait for them, or **Switch Now**.

Your first sign-in with access creates a Workspace for you, named after you,
with you as its owner. If you joined with a teammate's code, the first launch
opens their Workspace instead. See [Workspaces and
members](/cloud/workspaces/).

## Account status

Signed in, **Settings → Account** shows your **Name**, **Email** and a
**Volli Cloud** row:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Volli Cloud accepted this account |
| **Offline** | Volli Cloud is unreachable; your sign-in is kept |
| **Not accepted** | Volli Cloud did not accept this account; the notice below the row says why |

### Waitlisted

A waitlisted account reads **Not accepted**, with **Volli Cloud is
invite-only. You're on the waitlist; your local projects are unchanged.**

You stay signed in and Volli stays a local app. No Workspace is created and
no restart is offered. When you receive access later, sign out and sign in
again with the agreement checked.

### A new Preview Agreement

When the Preview Agreement changes, the status reads **Sign out and accept
the current Preview Agreement to continue.** Sign out, then sign in again with
the agreement checked. If Volli says **Update Volli to accept the current
Preview Agreement.**, update the app first.

## Sign out

In **Settings → Account**, select **Sign out**.

- From a local launch, the sign-in is forgotten at once.
- From a Volli Cloud launch, Volli asks **Sign out and restart Volli?** and
  then restarts into your local board. The cached Volli Cloud boards on this
  Mac are cleared.

If Volli Cloud stops accepting your sign-in while you are working, the status
bar shows **Signed out of Volli Cloud** with the action that recovers it:
**Sign in again** or **Restart Volli**.
